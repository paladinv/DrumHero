About "Human Hands with armature":

  • Blender: 2.79
  • Render Engine: Cycles
  • Uploaded on: 2018-11-22 15:46:18
Left and Right human hands with a certain degree of realism. Working armature with a control bone for each individual fingers apart from the thumb. Each bone can be moved independantly. Takes a bit of work to get a good hand pose because hands have so many quirky positions in wich they can be found. No texture provided.The mesh is on a different layer than the armature.The left hand is feminine looking and the right hand is male looking.
